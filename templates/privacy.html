1. Legal Basis and Purpose of Data Processing
GDPR Legal Bases
We process personal data based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest):
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR - Contract Performance
Processing necessary to provide URL shortening services you request. Providing a URL and accepting session cookies is required for service provision. Without this data, we cannot create or manage shortened links.
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR - Legitimate Interest
Security measures (rate limiting, abuse prevention, CAPTCHA), service optimization, and fraud prevention. Our legitimate interests are:
• Protecting service availability and preventing abuse
• Ensuring security for all users
• Detecting and preventing fraudulent activity

Balancing assessment: IP processing is minimal and transient (60 seconds), no persistent tracking occurs, strong encryption protects data, and security measures are proportionate to risks. These interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.
Data Subject Categories
We process personal data of the following categories of data subjects:
• Link Creators: Users who create shortened URLs
• Link Visitors: Users who access shortened URLs (minimal transient processing)
• Abuse Reporters: Users who submit abuse reports about links
Mandatory Data Provision (Art. 13(2)(e) GDPR)
Required for Service: URL to shorten, session cookie, IP address (transient processing)
Consequence of Non-Provision: Service cannot be provided without these data elements. Without a URL, no shortened link can be created. Without session cookies, link management is not possible. Without IP processing, security measures cannot function.
Optional: Encryption keys (stored client-side only), abuse report details
Legal Requirement: No statutory or contractual obligation to provide data. Provision is voluntary, but contractually necessary if you wish to use the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
2. Categories of Personal Data Processed
Information You Provide
Shortened URLs
The original URLs you submit for shortening. Optional client-side AES-256-GCM encryption with your key. Retained until you delete the link.
Encryption Keys (Optional)
Keys for encrypted links processed client-side only via PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations). Never stored on our servers—only in your browser's localStorage.
Abuse Reports
Data Collected: Link codes, abuse type, description
Source: Directly from reporting user (data subject) and indirectly about reported link creator (third party per Art. 14 GDPR)
Visibility: Reports accessible only via unique report ID (not publicly listed)
Retention: Pseudonymized after resolution by removing reporter identifiers; pseudonymized reports retained for reporter transparency
Sensitive Data: Decryption tokens cleared immediately after automated processing completes (whether link removed or moved to investigating status)
Third-Party Notification: Reported link creators are not individually notified due to disproportionate effort (Art. 14(5)(b) GDPR). Justification: No account system exists, no direct contact information is collected from link creators, no persistent user identity data is stored beyond session tokens (which do not contain identifying information), and technical identification of link creators would require collection of additional data constituting disproportionate effort. This privacy policy is publicly available to all users.
How Your Data Flows
Your Browser
Data encrypted client-side before transmission
Our Servers
Secure storage with HTTPS & HMAC tokens
Your Control
Delete anytime via management interface
Automatically Collected Information
IP Addresses (Personal Data under GDPR)
Purpose: Rate limiting, CAPTCHA difficulty adjustment, abuse prevention
Legal Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in service security)
Processing: Processed transiently in memory, hashed with SHA-256 for abuse reports
Retention: Not stored persistently in database; rate limit data held in memory expires after 60 seconds of inactivity, with periodic cleanup every 5 minutes (300 seconds) removing entries older than 1 hour
Session Data (Cookies)
Type: Technically necessary cookies (Art. 25 TTDSG)
Content: Cryptographically signed tokens (random nonce, timestamps)
Purpose: Associate shortened links with your browser session
Legal Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance)
Security: HMAC-SHA256 signed with constant-time comparison
Retention: Automatically rotates hourly (3600 seconds); maximum 30 days (2,592,000 seconds)
CAPTCHA & Security Tokens
CSRF tokens prevent cross-site attacks (1-hour/3600 second expiration). CAPTCHA tokens are AES-256-GCM encrypted challenges (5 minute/300 second expiration, cleaned up every 10 minutes/600 seconds). Clearance tokens reduce CAPTCHA frequency (1-hour/3600 second retention). Used CAPTCHA tokens tracked in memory (max 1000 entries) to prevent replay attacks.
Click Statistics
Number of times each link is accessed. Click statistics are stored without persistent IP addresses or user identifiers—just a simple counter incremented on each access. IP addresses may be processed transiently for rate limiting but are not stored with click data.
Third-Party Services (Optional)
Phishing Protection
Automated blocking using public blocklists (updated daily). All processing happens on our servers—no data sent to third parties for this feature.
Discord Webhooks (Optional Notification)
When: When automated checks cannot resolve an abuse report and status changes to "investigating" (if configured)
Data Sent: Report summaries (reason, description, defanged URLs, link count, no IP addresses, no decryption tokens)
Frequency: Automated processing runs every 30 seconds
Legal Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in abuse prevention)
Data Transfer: Transfer to USA; Discord uses Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by EU Commission
Safeguards: SCCs with supplementary measures, transfer impact assessment conducted
Privacy: Decryption tokens and personal identifiers never included

4. Technical and Organizational Security Measures
In accordance with Art. 32 GDPR, we implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk. These measures are designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access.
Encryption
Optional client-side encryption · HTTPS enforced with HSTS · Cryptographic token signing · Constant-time comparison prevents timing attacks
Input Validation
HTTPS-only URLs · Blocks self-referential, localhost, and IP addresses · Length limits and special character filtering · Strict JSON content-type checking
Security Headers
X-Content-Type-Options · X-Frame-Options · Content-Security-Policy · Referrer-Policy · Permissions-Policy · Cross-Origin policies
Database Security
Parameterized queries prevent SQL injection · Session-based authorization · Maximum limits enforced (1000 links per session, 100 bulk operations, 50 requests per session per minute)
5. Data Retention and Deletion
Personal data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Retention periods are as follows:
5 Minutes
CAPTCHA tokens expire (300 seconds); cleanup runs every 10 minutes (600 seconds)
1 Hour
Clearance tokens expire (3600 seconds) · Rate limit data cleared after 1 hour inactivity (cleanup every 5 minutes) · Session rotation occurs
30 Days
Session data maximum retention
Until You Delete / Resolution + 30 Days
Shortened links retained until you delete them—you have full control (max 1000 links per session) · Abuse reports: sensitive data (decryption tokens) cleared immediately after automated processing; reports pseudonymized after resolution (reporter identifiers removed); pseudonymized reports deleted 30 days after resolution
6. Your Rights Under GDPR
Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:
Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is being processed, and to access such data. View your links at /#manage.
Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
You have the right to obtain rectification of inaccurate personal data. Edit URLs for your shortened links via the management interface.
Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
You have the right to obtain erasure of personal data concerning you. Delete links individually or in bulk via /#manage. Clear cookies to reset your session.
Right to Restriction (Art. 18 GDPR)
You have the right to obtain restriction of processing under certain conditions. Contact us at ripplit@tn3w.dev to exercise this right.
Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
You have the right to receive personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. Contact us at ripplit@tn3w.dev to request data export in JSON format. This right applies to data processed based on consent or contract.
Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR)
You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We will cease processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or for establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims. Contact us at ripplit@tn3w.dev to exercise this right.
Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77 GDPR)
You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of alleged infringement. For Germany, you may contact your state's data protection authority. A list of German supervisory authorities is available at: BfDI - Data Protection Authorities
Exercising Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ripplit@tn3w.dev. We will respond to your request within one month. Many rights can be exercised directly through the management interface at /#manage.

10. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

No Profiling
